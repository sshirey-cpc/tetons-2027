Waterfalls

Mid-June is peak snowmelt, the loudest week of the year. Hidden Falls is on Saturday.

Snow

The high country is usually still snowbound. The low trails around Colter Bay are clear. Check with a ranger that week.

Wildlife

Calving season: moose, elk and bison are out with their young. Best near sunrise and sunset.

Fishing

The Snake runs high in June. Fish Jackson Lake and below the dam; the river clears by July.

Sat Jun 12

Hidden Falls by boat

Colter Bay to Jenny Lake: about 30 min

8:30 AM
Park early at Jenny Lake. Boat shuttle across the lake, a half mile to the 100-foot Hidden Falls, then another half mile to Inspiration Point.
Option
Strong hikers continue into Cascade Canyon, the classic Teton hike between the peaks, with good moose odds. Go as far as snow allows; the canyon forks are about 4.5 miles from the boat dock.
1:00 PM
Picnic at String Lake (5 min away), then walk the shore toward Leigh Lake: the Mount Moran reflection from the top of this page. Wade or paddle in the clear, shallow water.
Evening
Dinner at Jackson Lake Lodge, then Willow Flats from the terrace at dusk for moose.

Big activity: Snake River float

Colter Bay to the Moose float meeting point: about 45 min

Float group · takes the car
Morning
Scenic float, about 10 miles on the Snake with the whole range in view (2–2½ hours on the water). Eagles, moose and beavers along the banks.
Lunch
Dornan's in Moose, then the Chapel of the Transfiguration (a 1925 log chapel whose altar window frames the Tetons) and Menor's Ferry next door.
Afternoon
Pick a hike: Taggart Lake (about 3 miles), or Phelps Lake at the Rockefeller Preserve (about 3 miles to the shore, with the famous jumping rock farther along).
Drive home
Schwabacher Landing for mountain reflections in the beaver ponds, and Antelope Flats for bison.

Waterfalls

Sat Jun 12

Hidden Falls

The park's signature waterfall, a 100-foot cascade tucked into Cascade Canyon. Ride the boat across Jenny Lake, walk half a mile through the forest, and hear it before you see it. Keep going another half mile up to Inspiration Point for the view back over the lake.

Hidden Falls (NPS) · Jenny Lake boat shuttle

Getting there

The Jenny Lake boat

The shuttle runs continuously from the east dock, no reservations. It's about 12 minutes across, saving 2 miles of walking each way. Round trip runs about $20 for adults and $12 for kids. Go early; the Jenny Lake lot fills by mid-morning.

Why not Jackson

Straight to the cabins

The airport is 9 miles north of town and 50 minutes south of Colter Bay, so town nights mean backtracking. The only reasons to go into Jackson are a cheaper grocery run (about 20 minutes each way from the airport) and whitewater rafting, which works as a day trip later.
