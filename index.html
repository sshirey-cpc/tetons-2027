What mid-June changes

June 11 is early season. Most of the plan works as before, but snow and spring runoff shift a few things.

Snow

The high country is usually still snowbound. The Paintbrush–Cascade loop is typically snow-covered all of June, so the long Holly Lake hike is out. Lower Cascade Canyon, Hidden Falls, Inspiration Point and the low lake trails (Taggart, String Lake) are the right targets. Check with a ranger the week of the trip.

Camping

High backcountry camps will likely be under snow. For the people who want a night outside, plan on Colter Bay Campground, a short walk from the cabins.

Fishing

The Snake River is in runoff in June, so guided trips start on the clear stretch below Jackson Lake Dam and on Jackson Lake. The river itself usually clears by July.

Water

The lakes are open by June, but the water is snowmelt cold. Plan for short swims and wetsuit-free paddles close to shore.

Daylight

Long days: sunrise is around 5:40 AM and sunset around 9:10 PM. Wildlife is most active near both.

Itinerary

No rental car needed. Everything below is walkable or bookable from Colter Bay or downtown Jackson, with shuttles between them. Pink tags are optional, so people can split up and meet back for dinner.

Flights

There's no summer nonstop from DC or New Orleans to Jackson Hole, so both groups connect once. June 2027 flights are already on sale. Times below are from JAC's summer 2026 schedule and could shift for 2027.

From Washington DC · 3 people
  • Denver (United) from Dulles. Five Denver–JAC flights a day, so the most backups if anything runs late.
  • Dallas (American) from Reagan. Three DFW–JAC flights a day.
  • Chicago O'Hare on United or American.
  • Charlotte (American) is the fastest (about 6 h 20 min) but needs a very early start for the 8:55 AM JAC flight.
  • BWI is often the cheapest DC airport.
From New Orleans · 5 people
  • Dallas (American) is the most common connection, about 6 hours total.
  • Denver (United), about 7 flights a day from MSY.
  • Houston (United) is the quickest, but the IAH–JAC nonstop ran Fri/Sat only and returned Sat/Sun only in 2026. It fits the arrival, not a Thursday or Friday return.
  • Salt Lake (Delta) one nonstop a day from MSY, then four SLC–JAC flights.
Flight times that work with the shuttle
  • Arrival day: any time works, since the first night is in town (9 miles by taxi or shuttle).
  • Option A departure from Colter Bay: a private shuttle to JAC takes about 50 min, so any flight after about 11 AM is comfortable.
  • Option B departure from town: 15 min to the airport, so any flight.
  • In 2026, Dallas flights left JAC at 11:52 AM, 1:00 PM and 4:30 PM, and Denver had several a day. Each group books the cheapest option; no need to meet up.

Shuttle times are from Grand Teton Lodge Company's last published schedule (2019) and will change for 2027; confirm with the lodge.

Where to stay

Colter Bay Village is the pick. Headwaters and Signal Mountain are the alternates if Colter Bay is full or the group wants something different.

Pick · Colter Bay Village

307-543-3100 · gtlc.com · from about $299/night

Pros

  • Nice log cabins on Jackson Lake, inside the park
  • Two 2-room cabins (4 double beds each, 4 people per cabin) for about $6,000 for 6 nights including tax
  • Marina, horseback corral, campground, store and restaurants all walkable
  • 10 min to Oxbow Bend and Jackson Lake Lodge

Cons

  • No kitchen; simple furnishings
  • About 45 min to the Moose float, 50 min to the airport
Alternate · Headwaters Lodge at Flagg Ranch

307-543-2861 · gtlc.com · from about $354/night

Pros

  • About 5 min to Yellowstone's South Entrance, so the Yellowstone day gets much shorter
  • Quiet forest cabins with private baths; 2-queen cabins sleep up to 5, so two cabins fit 8
  • Horseback rides, fly fishing on the Snake River, Huckleberry Hot Springs nearby
  • Well reviewed (about 8.6/10); restaurant on site

Cons

  • About an hour to the Moose float and the airport; 30 min to Colter Bay and Two Ocean Lake
  • No kitchens; spotty Wi-Fi and cell service
  • Confirm it's fully open by June 10–12
Alternate · Signal Mountain Lodge

877-841-1076 · signalmountainlodge.com · from about $496/night

Pros

  • On Jackson Lake with its own marina: kayaks, canoes, motorboats, guided lake fishing
  • One "Home Away from Home" cabin sleeps 8 with a full kitchen
  • Lakefront units with Teton views; campground next door
  • Most central: about 35 min to Moose, 40 min to the airport

Cons

  • Most expensive of the three
  • Only one 8-person cabin, so it books early
  • Rustic cabins are simple and older

Also looked at: Jackson Lake Lodge (hotel-style, pricier), Jenny Lake Lodge (luxury, very expensive), Turpin Meadow Ranch and Togwotee Mountain Lodge (outside the park, longer drives).
